D+0: offices not in office master

Offices of the uploaded D+0 "table" file whose Office ID is not in the built-in master. For each one choose: (A) NEW office - select the division first, then the sub-division of that division, then the class, and press Save; or (B) Add its numbers to an existing office ID - write the office ID it belongs to and press Save. The reports are rebuilt immediately and the choice is remembered for the next uploads.

SB office not mapped

BO ID / SOL ID found in the uploaded POSB files but not in the mapping sheet. Until you fix it, its numbers are counted in the division written next to the code (sub-division “SB OFFICE NOT MAPPED”). Add it as a new office (name, class, sub-division) or add its numbers to another ID.

Nil Booking / office-wise settings and Mega PPT / PDF

Aadhaar machines and number of SOs (division-wise)

No. of SOs is pre-filled by counting the SOs of each division in the office mapping (old mapping → Office ID master, new mapping → New Office Mapping sheet). You can edit any number; it is kept until you press "Reset to office mapping". To use a different SO count for a particular day, edit the value in that day's row on the division tab of the report.

Prior-year baseline row

The baseline row of each month automatically shows the same month of the previous year (MO, Parcel, IRGB, Aadhaar transactions). Per-day average = Total Transactions ÷ Machines ÷ 25.

Unknown offices (Office ID → Division)

Columns: office-id and division-name. These entries override the built-in master.
Used only for the post-office-wise CSV (1B). An office whose division cannot be found is listed here – write its division and save; it is remembered for the next uploads.

Product → Category mapping

Products containing "International" are always counted as IRGB. Changing the mapping does not require re-uploading the CSV – the report is rebuilt immediately with the new mapping.
